How to Apply

1

Submit Application

Complete the application form with personal and academic information.

2

Submit Documents

Provide academic transcripts, birth certificate, and recommendation letters.

3

Entrance Exam

Take the entrance examination in Mathematics, English, and General Knowledge.

4

Interview

Selected candidates will be invited for an interview with parents/guardians.

5

Admission Decision

Receive your admission decision within two weeks of the interview.

Admission Requirements

New Students

  • Completion of Grade 8 with passing grades
  • Birth certificate or equivalent
  • Passport-size photographs (2)
  • Transfer certificate (if applicable)
  • Recommendation letter from previous school

Transfer Students

  • Academic transcript from current school
  • Transfer certificate
  • Recommendation from current principal
  • Interview with academic committee
  • Placement test
