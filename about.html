About Kersa Secondary School

Excellence in Education Since 1985

Our History

Kersa Secondary School was established in 1985 with a vision to provide quality secondary education to the community of Kersa district and surrounding areas. From humble beginnings with just 50 students and 5 teachers, we have grown into one of the leading secondary schools in the Oromia region.

Over the past 35+ years, we have educated thousands of students who have gone on to become doctors, engineers, teachers, entrepreneurs, and community leaders. Our commitment to academic excellence and holistic development has remained unwavering throughout our history.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality secondary education that empowers students with knowledge, skills, and values to become responsible citizens and lifelong learners.

Our Vision

To be a model secondary school in Ethiopia, recognized for academic excellence, innovation in teaching, and nurturing well-rounded individuals who contribute positively to society.

Our Values

Integrity, Excellence, Respect, Responsibility, Inclusivity, and Community Service form the foundation of everything we do at Kersa Secondary School.
